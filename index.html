About the Fest

Welcome to Smart Campus Fest 2026.

Smart Campus Fest 2026 is an inter-college technical, non-technical, and cultural event organized to bring together talented students from different colleges.

Includes technical events such as AI Quiz, Coding, Web Design, Paper Presentation, Debugging, Technical Connection, Problem Solving, Gaming, and Singing Competition.
